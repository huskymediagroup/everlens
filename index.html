NYC Micro-Wedding & Elopement FAQ

Intimate Wedding Photography & 4K Cinema Across NYC

How do you capture both photography and 4K video in one package?

Using a specialized hybrid Sony α7 III + DJI Ronin stabilizer rig and 32-bit float RODE Wireless PRO lapel audio, our team seamlessly alternates between high-resolution editorial portraits and stabilized 4K motion clips.

What NYC micro-wedding locations and venues do you cover?

We regularly cover Staten Island (Snug Harbor Cultural Center, Conference House Park, Historic Richmond Town, waterfront restaurants), Brooklyn (Park Slope, Brooklyn Bridge Park, DUMBO, Prospect Park Boathouse & private dining buyouts), and Manhattan City Hall.

How quickly do we receive our vertical teaser reel and photo gallery?

Couples booking The Cinematic Micro-Wedding ($2,950) receive their 9:16 Vertical Social Teaser Reel within 48 hours of the celebration, with the complete color-graded high-resolution online gallery and 4K film delivered within 14 days.

How do we reserve our wedding or elopement date?

Select your package ($1,600 Intimate Essentials for up to 3 hours or $2,950 Cinematic Micro-Wedding for up to 6 hours) and submit your date via our inquiry form to receive a custom timeline proposal, digital agreement, and 50% retainer invoice.
